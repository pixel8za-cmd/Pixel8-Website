What we cover

More than the cap-and-gown pose.

Graduation day is busy and the timing is rarely yours to choose. We plan around the ceremony, find the spots that work in the light you’ll actually have, and keep things moving.

01

Portraits on campus

The columns, the steps, the fountain — we find the spot that works with the light at the time your ceremony finishes, even when that light is terrible.

02

Family photos

Parents, siblings, grandparents. For many families this is the photo that ends up on the wall, so it gets proper time rather than a quick group line-up.

03

The candid moments

Cap in the air, the hug after the ceremony, friends finding each other in the crowd — the frames that tell the story of the day.

04

Quick, not rushed

Campuses are packed on graduation day. We work fast and calmly so you’re not kept waiting — and the results don’t look hurried.

The standard

Why the pictures come back better.

Lit properly, whatever the light

Harsh midday sun or a dim late afternoon after a long ceremony — off-camera lighting and prime lenses mean the portraits still come back clean and correctly exposed.

Editing by hand

Every delivered frame is graded individually for colour, skin tone and contrast, so the whole set looks like one considered body of work.

Reliable on the day

Questions

Before you book.

Can you shoot straight after the ceremony?

Yes — most graduation shoots happen on campus straight after the ceremony. Tell us the venue and the time and we’ll plan the locations around the light.

Can the whole family be in the photos?

Of course — that’s usually the point. Let us know who’s coming so we can plan the groupings and make sure nobody is left out.

How much does graduation photography cost?

It depends on how long you need us and how many people are involved. Send the date and venue and you’ll get a written quote.

When do we get the photos?

Previews reach you within the week. The full gallery arrives on the delivery date we agree with you up front.

What do we receive?

A private online gallery with full-resolution downloads, plus web-sized copies for sharing. Print files are available on request.
