The standard

Why the pictures come back better.

Lit properly, whatever the room

A dim reception hall and a harsh midday car park both come back clean, sharp and correctly exposed — prime lenses, off-camera lighting, and someone who reads a room before shooting it.

Editing by hand

No batch preset dumped over the set. Every delivered frame is graded individually for colour, skin tone and contrast, so the gallery holds together as one body of work.

Reliable on the day

We arrive early, we know the run sheet, and we carry backups of everything that matters. You get an agreed delivery date up front — and we hit it.

Questions

Before you book.

How much does wedding photography cost?

It depends mainly on how many hours of coverage you need and whether you’d like a second shooter. Send the date, the venue and your rough timings, and you’ll get a written quote.

Do you offer a second photographer?

Yes — a second shooter is available for full-day coverage, which helps when getting ready happens in two places at once, or when you want the ceremony covered from two angles.

What happens if it rains?

We plan for it. We look at covered options around the venue before the day, so if the weather turns we move rather than lose the portraits.

When do we get the photos?

Previews reach you within the week. The full gallery arrives on the delivery date we agree with you up front, before the wedding.

What do we receive?

A private online gallery with full-resolution downloads, plus web-sized copies for sharing. Print files are available on request.
