The people behind the pixels.

Why the pictures come back better.

Anyone can hold a camera at an event. The difference shows up two weeks later, when you open the gallery.

Lit properly, whatever the room

Prime lenses, off-camera lighting and someone who reads a room before shooting it — so a dim reception hall and a harsh midday car park both come back clean, sharp and correctly exposed.

Editing by hand

No batch preset dumped over the set. Every delivered frame is graded individually for colour, skin tone and contrast, so the gallery holds together as one body of work.

Reliable on the day

We arrive early, we know the run sheet, and we carry backups of everything that matters. You get an agreed delivery date up front — and we hit it.

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. Step 01

    Enquire

    Tell us the date, the place and roughly what you need. You get a written quote — not a “let’s chat about budget”.

  2. Step 02

    Plan

    We agree the shot list, the timings and the locations before the day, so nothing important gets improvised.

  3. Step 03

    Shoot

    We turn up early, set up out of the way, and direct where it helps. Previews reach you within the week.

  4. Step 04

    Deliver

    A private online gallery with full-resolution downloads, plus web-sized copies. Print files on request.
