What we cover

The whole send-off, not just the posed shot.

Most matric dance shoots start at home, with a full house and a tight schedule. We plan around it so the important frames happen calmly and nobody’s photos get rushed.

01

Getting ready at home

Final touches, corsages, parents fixing ties. We work quietly around the pre-drinks and the full house, so the candid moments stay candid.

02

The send-off and the cars

The car arriving, the walk out, the family watching from the driveway. For joint send-offs we plan the order in advance so every couple gets their moment.

03

Couple portraits

Directed properly, so you don’t have to know what to do with your hands — and lit so the result still looks like you, whatever the light is doing.

04

Group and family photos

Friends, parents, grandparents. For group bookings we agree the shot list with every family beforehand, so everyone leaves with the photos they wanted.

The standard

Why the pictures come back better.

Lit properly, whatever the light

Send-offs happen when the sun is low or already gone. Off-camera lighting and prime lenses mean a driveway at dusk still comes back clean, sharp and correctly exposed.

Editing by hand

No batch preset dumped over the set. Every delivered frame is graded individually for colour, skin tone and contrast, so the gallery holds together.

Reliable on the day

We arrive early, we know the timings, and we carry backups of everything that matters. You get an agreed delivery date up front — and we hit it.

Questions

Before you book.

How much does matric dance photography cost?

It depends on how long you need us and whether it’s one family or a group booking. Send the date, the send-off address and roughly what you have in mind, and you’ll get a written quote — not a “let’s chat about budget”.

Can several families share one booking?

Yes. Joint send-offs are common, and we’ve handled six families on one very tight schedule. We plan the shot list and timings with everyone beforehand so no one’s photos get squeezed.

Do you come to our house for the send-off?

Yes — most matric dance shoots start at home or wherever the send-off is happening. We arrive early, set up out of the way, and work around the pre-drinks.

When do we get the photos?

Previews reach you within the week. The full gallery arrives on the delivery date we agree with you up front, before the shoot.

What do we receive?

A private online gallery with full-resolution downloads, plus web-sized copies for sharing. Print files are available on request.
